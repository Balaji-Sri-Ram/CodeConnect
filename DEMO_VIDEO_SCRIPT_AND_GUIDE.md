# CodeConnect Demo Video: Complete 4m 45s Production Script & Recording Guide

This guide provides the exact second-by-second walkthrough, voiceover narration script, and screen recording cues to record the official 4 to 5-minute product demo video for the **CodeConnect** landing page.

---

## ⏱️ Video Overview & Timing Structure (Total: 4m 45s)

| Chapter | Time | Screen / Feature | Voiceover Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | `0:00 - 0:45` | **Student Dashboard & Stats** | Login welcome, daily streak, solved counter, weekly velocity, quick action cards. |
| **02** | `0:45 - 1:35` | **Problems Catalog & Filters** | 500+ algorithmic questions, Easy/Medium/Hard tags, Company tags, CodeCoin rewards. |
| **03** | `1:35 - 2:50` | **Code Editor, Live Run & Verdict** | Syntax highlighting, language switcher, test cases, live execution, Accepted verdict, reward coins. |
| **04** | `2:50 - 3:35` | **Global Leaderboard** | Real-time global developer rankings, university benchmarks, seasonal points. |
| **05** | `3:35 - 4:05` | **Top Performer Profile** | Verified skill badges, rating metrics, submission heatmap, recruiter inquiry view. |
| **06** | `4:05 - 4:25` | **Companies & Direct Hiring** | Sponsoring tech companies, direct placement challenges, compensation tiers. |
| **07** | `4:25 - 4:45` | **User Profile, Wallet & Call to Action** | Personal resume status, coin redemption, certificates, signup invitation. |

## 🎙️ Word-for-Word Voiceover Script & Screen Cues

### Chapter 1: Post-Login Dashboard & Live Stats (`0:00 - 0:45`)
- **Visual Action:** Start on the CodeConnect authenticated dashboard (`/dashboard`). Mouse smoothly pans over the stats cards and streak counter.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Welcome to CodeConnect! Once you sign in, you arrive at your personalized developer headquarters. Right at the top, you get an instant overview of your coding consistency: your active streak, total problems solved across Easy, Medium, and Hard, and your current CodeCoins balance. Below, the weekly velocity chart tracks your daily progress, keeping you motivated and interview-ready. With one click, you can resume your last problem or jump straight into active company challenges."*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Welcome to CodeConnect! Track your daily streak, solved problems, and earned CodeCoins in real time.`

---

### Chapter 2: Curated Problems Catalog (`0:45 - 1:35`)
- **Visual Action:** Navigate to the Problems page (`/all-problems`). Scroll through the list, toggle the filter for "Medium", and select the "Google" tag.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Next, let's explore our curated Problem Vault. CodeConnect hosts hundreds of industry-standard coding challenges designed by engineers from top tech companies. You can quickly filter challenges by difficulty—Easy, Medium, or Hard—or target specific topics like Dynamic Programming, Graphs, and Trees. Each question displays its community acceptance rate, difficulty badge, and the exact CodeCoins rewarded upon completion."*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Filter 500+ industry problems by difficulty, company tags, and data structure categories.`

---

### Chapter 3: In-Browser Code Editor & Live Execution (`1:35 - 2:50`)
- **Visual Action:** Click into a problem (e.g., "Two Sum"). Show problem statement, test cases tab, and editor (`/compiler` or problem editor). Type/paste clean Python or C++ code. Click "Run Code", watch terminal execute, and show "Accepted" verdict modal.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Clicking on any problem opens our lightning-fast in-browser IDE. There is zero local setup required. Select your favorite programming language—from Python and C++ to Java and JavaScript. The editor features full syntax highlighting, intelligent auto-indentation, and customizable test inputs. Once your logic is ready, hit 'Run Code'. In milliseconds, our sandboxed compiler tests your solution against edge cases. All test cases passed! You get an instant Accepted verdict, complete with runtime speed percentiles and bonus CodeCoins credited directly to your profile."*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Write and run code in multiple languages with sub-second cloud execution and instant test verdicts.`

---

### Chapter 4: Global Leaderboard & Rankings (`2:50 - 3:35`)
- **Visual Action:** Navigate to `/leaderboard`. Scroll through Top 10 coders with gold/silver/bronze trophies. Show weekly/all-time filters.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Ready to see how you rank? Head over to the Global Leaderboard. Here, developers from universities and bootcamps around the world compete in seasonal coding sprints. You can filter rankings globally, by university, or by active sprint. Every problem solved, contest won, and streak sustained boosts your global rank, making your profile visible to elite tech recruiters."*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Climb the real-time Global Leaderboard and showcase your ranking to leading tech recruiters.`

---

### Chapter 5: Top Performer Verified Profile (`3:35 - 4:05`)
- **Visual Action:** Click on the #1 ranked profile (or `/developer/:id`). Show verified skills, submission heatmap, rating graph, and badges.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Recruiters and peers can click on any top performer to view their verified public portfolio. Unlike traditional resumes, CodeConnect profiles showcase verified code quality, submission consistency heatmaps, and skill badges earned through pure merit. It provides undeniable proof of engineering excellence."*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Verified developer profiles display genuine problem-solving metrics and skill credentials.`

---

### Chapter 6: Companies & Hiring Challenges (`4:05 - 4:25`)
- **Visual Action:** Navigate to `/companies`. Show company cards (Google, Microsoft, startups), open challenges, salary ranges, and "Apply with Score".
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Through our Companies hub, top tech employers sponsor direct hiring challenges. Skip the resume black hole—qualify for high-paying roles simply by solving the company's benchmark challenge with your CodeConnect performance."*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Connect directly with top tech firms hiring on pure merit and verified coding performance.`

---

### Chapter 7: User Profile, Wallet & Sign-Off (`4:25 - 4:45`)
- **Visual Action:** Open the user's Profile (`/profile`). Show coin redemption store, earned certificates, resume upload status, then return to landing page hero.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Your personal profile holds your earned certificates, resume status, and coin store where you can unlock platform perks. Start coding for free today, build your proof of work, and connect with top tech companies at CodeConnect!"*
- **Subtitle (Bottom-Center):**
  `Your journey from student to professional starts here. Join CodeConnect today!`

## 🎬 How to Add Your Recorded Video File

Once you have recorded your MP4 video:
1. Name the video file: `demo-video.mp4`
2. Place it directly into your project's `public/` directory:
   `c:\Users\ramup\OneDrive\Desktop\CodeConnect\public\demo-video.mp4`
3. The demo player on the landing page will automatically detect it and offer a toggle between your **Recorded Video** and the **Interactive Tour**!
